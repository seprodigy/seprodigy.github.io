About Me

I am a senior undergraduate in Automation at Tsinghua University, pursuing a B.Eng. in Control Science and Engineering. I work as a research assistant with Prof. Jianyu Chen at Tsinghua and collaborate with Prof. Kuan Fang at Cornell University through summer research.

I am broadly interested in all aspects of embodied AI. My current research focuses on dexterous manipulation: how robots can learn useful skills from limited data and act reliably in the real world. I work on skill transfer from human demonstrations and videos to robots, policy training and evaluation, and generalizable world action models for long horizon tasks.

I am seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.

Research Interests

  • Dexterous manipulation: Skill transfer, policy training & evaluation
  • Sim-to-real transfer: Evaluation & generalization
  • World action models: Generalization & long horizon tasks

Weighted dexterous task success: 45% → 80%

arXiv preprint ↗
Abstract

Veo-Act combines high-level video planning with low-level vision-language-action control. A trained dual-head inverse dynamics model extracts actions from video and enables switching between navigation, coarse manipulation, and fine interaction.
